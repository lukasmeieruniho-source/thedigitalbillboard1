FAQ

WHAT IS THIS?

This is a pixelbased advertising website where individuals, creators, and businesses can purchase digital space to promote their brand, product, or online presence.

The homepage is divided into a grid of pixels. Each pixel can be bought and used as advertising space. You can display your logo, image, or message and also include a clickable link to your website or social media.

This means every visitor can directly click on your ad and be redirected to your page, making it a simple and effective way to gain visibility online.

HOW MUCH DOES A PIXEL COST?

Each pixel costs $10. You can buy as many pixels as you want, depending on how large you want your advertisement to appear.

For better visibility, I would recommend purchasing multiple pixels to create a larger image or banner. The bigger your space, the more attention your ad will attract.

HOW DO I BUY PIXELS?

To purchase pixels, simply select the available (free) pixels on the grid and follow the purchase instructions.

After your purchase, your ad will be manually reviewed and then published on the homepage within a short time.

We ensure that every customer receives their purchased space. All orders are tracked and confirmed, and your pixels will be reserved exclusively for you once the payment is completed.

This project is transparent, and you will always be able to see your ad live on the website. If you have any issues, support is available to help you at any time.

HOW DO I GET INTO THE HALL OF FAME?

If you purchase 100 pixels or more, you will automatically be featured in our exclusive Hall of Fame.

The Hall of Fame highlights top supporters and advertisers, giving you additional visibility on the website.

Your name, brand, or project will be displayed in a special section, making your ad stand out even more compared to regular placements.

This is a great way to gain extra attention and be part of the most prominent spots on the platform.
